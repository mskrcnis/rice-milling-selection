# Revision scope and exposure

Revision opened 20 November 2026 after the original held-out study was completed. All original outcomes are now known. Every new analysis is a post hoc diagnostic, including precision perturbations, detailed action comparisons and the breeder-facing rank display. These diagnostics cannot replace the original primary comparison, cohorts, fitting cutoff, models, thresholds, exclusions, tie rules or budgets.

The source project is read-only: ../paper1_rice_milling_selection. Its 552-file release manifest passed at revision entry. The unrelated AWD project is not used. All new files are written in this sibling directory. Diagnostic plans and seeds are fixed before the new calculations, but that does not make them preregistered or confirmatory.

The intended submission claim concerns within-program selection on a source-basis yield times head-rice-recovery index. Exact top-entry identification and loss in shortlist value are distinct outcomes. Absolute output is not compared between countries. No physical assay counts, monetary savings, prospective validation or causal national/program explanation is inferred.

The revised manuscript will remove internal draft/status prose, retain author-supplied declarations privately until verified, use current Crop Science formatting, and include a referenced supplement and local reviewer reproducibility bundle. No correspondence, repository publication or submission is authorized or performed.
