# Post hoc descriptive breeder figure

**Main figure: `breeder_field_yield_ranks.pdf` (also PNG and SVG).** Field-yield ranks of the entries in each pool's full-testing top-decile shortlist, defined by the original source-basis paddy-yield × head-rice-recovery index. Field-yield rank is descending among eligible candidates with observed field yield and milling recovery; ties use the original fixed hash. Each pool contributes equal total weight, divided among its k=ceil(0.10n) reference entries. The horizontal coordinate is the entry's field-yield rank divided by that pool's exact quarter-budget query count, B=ceil(0.25n); one therefore marks the rounded 25% budget in every pool. It is not a percentage rank. At that boundary, yield-first recovers a mean 93.93% of reference entries in 188 Uruguay pools and 67.74% in 62 Arkansas pools. Thin lines show the four Uruguay and nine Arkansas harvest-year curves; they are descriptive, with recurring sites, and are not uncertainty intervals. All curves use frozen reported values and do not incorporate the separate rounding sensitivity. No national or biological cause is inferred.

**Denominator companion: `breeder_denominator_comparison.png` (also SVG).** The left panel uses one-based descending rank divided by the observed-answer candidate count. The right panel preserves the original predictive feature: one minus the ascending average-rank percentile among all compatible candidates with observed field yield, including candidates without a milling answer. The underlying field/eligible counts are 7,596/7,568 in Uruguay and 1,715/1,611 in Arkansas. Average ties and different denominators mean the right coordinate is not a sequential query position; neither a 25th percentile nor a fixed vertical rank fraction reproduces the ceiling-rounded quarter budget for every pool. The main figure uses the exact budget denominator to avoid that ambiguity. Curves retain equal pool weights.

**Coverage-period companion: `breeder_arkansas_periods.png` (also SVG).** The same post hoc rank display separates 19 historical-answer Arkansas pools (2017–2020; 447 eligible entries among 551 observed-field entries) and 43 contemporaneous pools (2021–2025; all 1,164 observed-field entries paired). Their quarter-budget mean recalls are 82.46% and 61.24%. Pool counts, source coverage, years and candidate composition change jointly. This is a descriptive source-period comparison, not a causal explanation or newly independent confirmation.

Figure source data: `reference_entry_field_yield_ranks.csv` has all 1,053 reference-entry records (869 Uruguay, 184 Arkansas), exact eligible rank, both denominators, rounded query count, year and pool. `pool_equal_ecdf_data.csv` contains the rendered program, period and year curves. `rank_display_summary.csv` distinguishes equal-pool recall from pooled reference-entry recall. An independent reconstruction confirms the marked quarter-budget values equal the original yield-first per-pool recall.
