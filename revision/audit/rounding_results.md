# Post hoc Arkansas precision diagnostic

The diagnostic preserves the original cohorts, policies, endpoints, query budgets and published-value results. Its plan, seed, source hashes and code were locked before this computation, after the original outcomes were known. It assesses representation by integer Arkansas paddy yields and head-recovery percentages, using closed intervals of ±0.5 bu/ac and ±0.5 percentage point. These intervals do not represent field sampling error or laboratory repeatability. Original project files were read only.

## Methods and interpretation

For each original yield-first tested set Q, the final shortlist contains the best k measured output products in Q and the reference contains the best k in the complete observed-answer pool. Increasing a tested product cannot decrease recall or increase absolute or relative regret; increasing an untested product has the opposite effect. Consequently, tested-low/untested-high and tested-high/untested-low corners attain the sharp worst and best fixed-query values. Each positive product endpoint is attainable within the specified yield/recovery rectangle. These are pointwise bounds for each budget's fixed Q. Different budgets generally require different extremal realizations, so the envelopes do not describe a single attained curve or bound a policy that changes its queries.

The separate joint sensitivity recomputes yield-first queries and scores from the **same** perturbed yield. Each candidate realization is reused across all six budgets. It contains 20,000 predeclared uniform feasible draws (master seed 20261120, fixed pool-specific PCG64 streams) and 16 symmetric deterministic scenarios: four global corners plus favorable/adverse corners targeted at each of six budgets, with query order then recomputed. Uniform offsets are a transparent scenario design, not an estimated error distribution. Their percentiles and observed extrema are neither confidence intervals nor guaranteed joint bounds. No scenario was selected for a favorable outcome. All summary metrics equally weight the original pools; draws do not add biological replicates. Uruguay remains an unchanged reference, with no assumed precision interval or pooled absolute mass comparison.

The full-precision primary recall gap is **26.1849798 percentage points**, rounding to 26.18; subtracting displayed 93.93 and 67.74 instead gives 26.19. Use 26.18 for a calculation from unrounded results, or 26.2 in prose. Both lower Arkansas identity recovery and larger relative regret persist in every examined joint scenario. This does not establish the extrema over all possible joint-query realizations. Contemporary complete coverage also does not remove the contrast.

## First measured-grid crossing of 90% mean recall

At reported values the first crossing is 60% for all 62 Arkansas pools, 40% for historical pools and 60% for contemporary pools (Uruguay: 20%). For all 62, the fixed-query upper envelope first crosses at 40% and the lower envelope at 60%; these describe possible/guaranteed crossings on the measured grid conditional on fixed queries. All 20,000 joint uniform scenarios first cross at 60%. However, **one of 16 predeclared deterministic joint scenarios first crosses at 40%**, attaining 90.323% recall there; the other 15 cross at 60%. Thus “60% is required” is not a universal rounding-robust claim. Say “the reported-value curve first reached 90% on the tested grid at 60%.” Historical and contemporary crossings remain 40% and 60%, respectively, in all examined joint scenarios. No exhaustive joint-query guarantee was computed for either subgroup.

## Exact ties and verification

The new diagnostic orders native yield × recovery products before applying a constant mass conversion and retains the frozen SHA tie rule. In the original ARPT 2021 GRE pool, CLM04 (180 × 67) and DGL274 (201 × 60) both equal 12,060; binary conversion creates a difference near 2 × 10⁻¹² in the original mass products. Canonical native-product ranking changes one pool's recall by one third at the **60% budget only**, with negligible regret difference. It has no primary 25% effect and does not change a grid crossing. Original results remain untouched; [native_float_tie_audit.csv](../outputs/rounding/native_float_tie_audit.csv) records this distinction.

Verification checked 372 original query sets, 32 exhaustive synthetic vertices and 29,016 independent scalar metric calculations (maximum discrepancy 2.79 × 10⁻¹²). A separate script using dictionaries, Python sorting and `math.fsum`, without importing either replay or diagnostic implementation, checked 2,976 pool decisions and 432 aggregate metrics (maximum discrepancy 2.28 × 10⁻¹³). Full-budget recall is 100% and regret zero. A fresh execution reproduced all nine diagnostic files byte for byte. See [independent scalar check](rounding_independent_scalar_check.json), [repeat check](rounding_repeat_check.json), and [locked plan](../protocol/rounding_diagnostic_plan.json).
