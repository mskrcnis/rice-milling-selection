# Dated analysis protocol: budgeted rice milling selection

Protocol date:20 November2026. This document is fixed before opening comparative temporal or external evaluation results. It supersedes provisional implementation details in `design_before_data.md` where specified below. The immutable `analysis_lock.json` will bind this file, selected development configuration, source/cohort/split hashes and all scientific code before the first evaluation. Source reviewers are outcome-exposed; policy development is outcome-separated, not whole-team blinded.

## Question and observable target

At a decision immediately after harvest and before milling, can two batches of entry testing reduce shortlist regret relative to the strongest one-batch policy at the same25% entry budget? Does paid first-batch feedback, isolated by an identical-first-batch no-update comparison, improve the selection? Does the locked source-program policy transfer to a separate program?

The unit is one distinct line/cultivar in one source-defined compatible trial at one site in one harvest year. Source trial IDs separate planting experiments and stages; germplasm classes do not manufacture additional pools. Multiple field replicates and repeated checks are aggregated to one candidate using arithmetic means of available measured values after documented exact-duplicate handling. Nonidentical source rows are retained, not silently deduplicated by an incomplete key. Missing outcomes are never imputed as truth. A paid query reveals the candidate-level measured milling result; it is not a measured count of physical laboratory assays or a financial cost.

The operational value is candidate mean paddy yield times candidate mean head-rice percentage divided by100. Arkansas explicitly defines the denominator as original rough rice and reports45lb/bushel at12% moisture; mass conversion is `bu/ac*45*0.45359237/0.40468564224`. Uruguay reports kg/ha and percentage whole milled kernels. Same-program primary methods support a rough-rice denominator, but archive-wide confirmation and yield moisture convention are absent. Therefore Uruguay's value is a source-basis whole-kernel-output index with that documented interpretation assumption. Absolute output is reported within its source basis; no moisture-harmonized mass comparison between programs is made. Cross-program comparisons use each pool's regret as a percentage of its own positive oracle mean. A common pool-wide moisture conversion would cancel in this percentage; unrecorded entry-specific differences would not.

The product of two means is distinct from the mean of plotwise products. Matched Uruguay plot records support a prespecified definition sensitivity, restricted to complete matched-answer pools without imputation. This observational replay does not identify genetic causal effects, future farmer risk or laboratory time savings.

## Evidence eligibility and splits

The source is the checksum-exact2023 Dryad INIA release, acquired through its public Dryad-community Zenodo mirror. All four required files match the publisher's SHA-256. ReData is not substituted: its anonymized columns lack verified trait definitions. Arkansas reports are screened as final annual source reports through the search date; preliminary2026 results, statewide/multiyear averages, planting-date experiments and duplicated editions are excluded from the main extraction. Historical cells may fill an answer only when the year, contemporary candidate identity and site are unambiguous. Every eligible candidate is anchored to a contemporaneous field-entry list. Ambiguous aliases and site-year headers remain missing rather than fabricated.

Primary pools require at least10 candidates with valid positive observed field yields and observed head recovery in0–100%. Public within-pool yield ratios and percentiles are computed using **all compatible observed-field entries before restricting to candidates with milling answers**. Source-specific paired availability and differences in public field attributes of included/excluded entries are reported. The replay target is conditional on the observed-answer subset; it cannot establish utility for the excluded candidates.

Uruguay initial fitting uses1997–2011 (2002 absent); rolling-origin development uses2012,2013,2014,2015,2016. Every development fit uses all eligible strictly earlier years. The latest suitable contiguous years2017–2020 form untouched temporal evaluation. Metadata-only coverage determined these cutoffs: initial454 pools/16,313 candidates; development273 pools/10,542 candidates; temporal188 pools/7,568 candidates. The final model is fit once on all source data through2016 and remains fixed for every evaluation year. No earlier test-year outcomes enter later-year histories or refits. Entire pools, candidates and every replicate remain together.

Arkansas is held apart during method selection. Primary transfer uses compatible2017–2025 pools, strictly later than the2016 source-model cutoff; earlier years remain audited pre-cutoff evidence and do not enter primary transfer. The entire previously illustrated2025 NERREC/Poinsett pool is exposed secondary evidence. Source-flagged compromised pools are retained in a separate secondary stratum:2022 CLAY/LAW weed pressure,2022 PTRS low harvest moisture,2024 ARK volunteer rice,2024 NEREC bird damage, and2025 RREC delayed harvest. Lodging alone is a recorded field attribute, not an outcome-selected exclusion. Final exact counts and row IDs are in the immutable cohort/split manifest. No external refit or local-adaptation model is part of the primary analysis; inability to transfer will not trigger post-score adaptation.

## Information, budgets and model selection

All trained core predictors and feedback similarities use the same ordinary cross-program public features: yield divided by the pool's field-yield mean and within-pool field-yield percentile. Uruguay germplasm/quality classes are not mapped onto Arkansas market classes. The core `grain_class` is the explicit constant `unharmonized`; original source classes remain namespaced metadata. The simple source-class-history comparator uses earlier source quality-class averages; an unseen external namespace falls back to the overall historical recovery mean. No phenotype recorded by the milling process, current/future cultivar description, pedigree, marker, future weather or hidden answer enters public features.

For n observed-answer candidates, k=ceil(.10n), B=min(n,max(k,ceil(fn))). Primary f=.25; secondary f=.10,.20,.40,.60,1.00. First-batch size=ceil(B/2), with a one-batch fallback at B=1. Final shortlist=best measured k among queried entries. Exact value ties are resolved by SHA-256 of `rice-milling-tie-v1|row_id`, identically for policy and oracle. The target90% oracle top-k recall is illustrative and assessed only on the measured budget grid, without interpolation or claims of savings. Top-ten recovery is separately reported among tested entries where n and B are at least10.

Static policies are random testing (seeds0–19), paddy-yield-first, source-class historical recovery, a Gaussian recovery mixed model with a random trial-pool intercept, ridge (alpha1,10,100), and extra trees (128 trees; minimum leaf5 or20; seed91021). Imputation/scaling/encoding are fit only on historical training data. Mixed-model variances use restricted marginal likelihood. Forecast uncertainty used for exploration is a historical variability heuristic; it is not a calibrated posterior interval. Failed fits and numerical diagnostics are retained.

The strongest static policy minimizes equal-pool mean percentage regret at25% on development pools; alphabetical name resolves a tie. The adaptive predictor is similarly selected among the mixed, ridge and tree models. Adaptive tuning considers uncertainty fractions.25,.50,.75 and residual-shrinkage constants2,8, only for that selected predictor. No budget or hyperparameter is chosen from final outcomes. The selected configuration and full development comparisons are frozen before final evaluation; final runs refit fixed models on all source data through2016.

Batch one contains exploitation selections by predicted value and uncertainty selections near the estimated kth-value boundary. At least one exploitation entry is selected. With predicted value m_i and scale s_i, the uncertainty score is `s_i*phi((m_i-t)/s_i)`, where t is the kth predicted value. It is reduced by one plus similarity to entries already selected, using a Gaussian kernel of relative yield (bandwidth.25). All batch-one choices precede its first reveal. For each untested j, batch-one paid residuals update recovery by

`delta_j = sum_i w_ij*(observed_HR_i-predicted_HR_i)/(lambda+sum_i w_ij)`.

Weights use the same relative-yield kernel; the class multiplier is1 when the common constant matches (and therefore always1 in the primary analysis). Updated recovery is clipped to0–100, a prespecified physical bound. Batch two ranks updated expected value. The no-update ablation shares the exact first batch, predictor and budget, with delta=0. An exploit-only first batch removes uncertainty sampling. All untested rank changes are recorded; a stable rank is reported even if it makes feedback ineffective.

## Evaluation and uncertainty

Primary outcome is shortlist regret: oracle mean of the best k observed values minus mean of the best k tested values. The primary contrast is two-batch minus the locked strongest static policy at25%; negative differences in regret favor two-batch. The critical mechanistic contrast is two-batch minus no-update, with the same sign. Native-source regret and percent-of-oracle regret are both retained; percentage regret is primary for cross-program interpretation. Secondary metrics are top-k recall, best entry found, tested counts/fractions, top-ten recovery and the measured budget needed for90% recall. Recovery prediction errors are diagnostic.

Each pool has equal weight. Random seeds are first averaged inside each pool and are not independent replicates. Paired differences always compare identical pools and costs. Percentile intervals use4,000 harvest-year block resamples with seed1731. With four Uruguay years these are explicitly descriptive; Arkansas year intervals are also conditional on the observed years/program and do not eliminate recurring-site dependence or temporal change. Per-year effects and leave-one-year/leave-one-site sensitivity accompany averages. No equivalence claim or stakeholder-approved practical margin is assumed. Source-class/stage, answer-availability and new/returning-line descriptions are retained without selecting favorable subgroups. Deterministic source-rounding intervals identify rank ambiguity; they are not laboratory sampling confidence intervals.

## Verification, exposure and completion

A query-gated policy gets only public features and paid answers. The immutable evaluation freeze binds fitted models, all initial forecasts, all deterministic initial batch plans, configuration, code and answer-key byte hashes before the first modeling evaluation opens labels. Adaptive second batches cannot be frozen independently of their paid feedback, so the algorithm and initial state are frozen instead. Later clean runs verify reproducibility, not fresh blind confirmation.

Tests cover budget/query limits, joins, units/percent denominators, PDF footnotes, duplicates, missing labels, temporal leakage, forbidden features, public normalization, row alignment and full-budget oracle identity. Mutating unqueried labels must not change earlier actions. An independent implementation regenerates primary per-pool metrics from recorded events, then aggregate tables are regenerated from those metrics. Two fresh fits/replays must agree. Source-specific independent extraction checks and one independently reconstructed pool are required. All outputs, failed attempts and deviations remain auditable.

Sequential phenotyping, top-genotype selection and temporal historical validation have prior art. Any supported contribution is specific to this postharvest entry-testing decision, matched feedback ablation and independent-program comparison. Positive, negative or inconclusive results receive the same fixed analysis; no retuning, favorable subgroup selection or additional outcome-driven acquisition is permitted after evaluation.

## Development-only amendment before evaluation

After the complete development grid was executed, the primary rule selected the mixed model (tied with yield and ridge at mean regret0.2836996%) and uncertainty fraction0.25/shrinkage2. The entire36-configuration adaptive development grid was already available. Before opening any temporal or external comparative outcomes, a secondary jointly selected configuration was added: extra trees with minimum leaf20, uncertainty fraction0.25 and shrinkage8 (development regret0.2831868%). Its advantage in development is tiny and selected from a larger grid, so it is not evidence of improvement. The original mixed-model primary is preserved. The secondary `adaptive_grid` is always reported with its own identical-first-batch `adaptive_grid_no_update` and the same locked static benchmark. It cannot replace the primary according to test performance. Both secondary policies use all prespecified budgets and no further tuning.

Arkansas2017–2020 answers are selectively recoverable from explicitly labeled historical cells in later reports;2021–2025 provide contemporaneous site-specific grain and milling matrices. A prespecified2021–2025-only external sensitivity reports the primary paired effects separately. It tests this source-coverage distinction without modifying the primary2017–2025 cohort.

Descriptive diagnostics summarize entire pools by source stage/class composition and observed-answer coverage, without constructing easier class-specific or stage-specific selection tasks. New/returning line statistics describe the oracle membership and recovery of individual lines under the original complete-pool decisions; returning means the same source-coded candidate appeared in an earlier eligible archive year, including earlier evaluation years for descriptive identification only. These flags are never model features or outcome histories; all fitted recovery histories remain restricted to training years through2016. Prediction RMSE, MAE and coarse calibration exclude the yield-only rank surrogate (whose artificial recovery100 is not a fitted forecast). No diagnostic subgroup becomes a confirmatory endpoint.
