# Arkansas source and extraction audit

The source frame is the official University of Arkansas rice archive and FINAL annual Rice Information Sheet / Arkansas Rice Performance Trials reports recovered for 2011–2025. All 15 annual files were screened before policy evaluation. Official archive links, variety-testing links, publication archives, and targeted official-domain searches were checked for earlier and later editions. Standalone 2008–2010 final annual files were not recovered; this is an access finding, not proof that they never existed. As accessed on 20 November 2026, the recovered 2026 update explicitly calls its results preliminary. It is retained as acquisition evidence and excluded. No future or unavailable final report is assumed.

`raw/arkansas/source_manifest.json` records URLs, acquisition times, file hashes, successful downloads, and failed requests. Existing raw files are never overwritten. The public site provides access but no open redistribution license was inferred; attribution and the source disclaimer are retained. Research Series volumes are methods corroboration only: their expanded or revised cultivar panels are not merged with the annual FINAL source values.

## Candidate identity and incomplete historical labels

The candidate universe is the contemporaneous annual ARPT grain table for each site and year. Every listed candidate is retained, including missing field yield or missing milling. Keys are `ARPT__year__site__candidate`, with cultivar names normalized only for case, punctuation, and spacing. No biological pedigree, renamed cultivar, or commercial/experimental alias is inferred. The site abbreviations NBES→NEC and PTBS→PTRS refer to the same named stations; NEREC and NERREC remain distinct.

The 2021–2025 annual reports contain matching current-year site grain and milling matrices. Earlier reports provide current-year site grain but pooled milling; pooled milling is never assigned to a site. Explicitly labelled individual-year columns in 2016–2025 site tables can supply historical milling for an already-listed candidate. Their repeated-year and across-site means are never observations. Historical labels are incomplete and may be selective because later tables retain selected cultivars. This limits interpretation of 2017–2020 pools and motivates a separate 2021–2025 contemporaneous-label stratum. Missing milling is not imputed; relabelled later cultivars do not create additional candidates.

A contemporaneous annual cell takes precedence even when it is missing. Otherwise the earliest report with an observed individual-year cell is selected. Other occurrences, including conflicts, remain in `private_duplicate_audit.csv`; values are neither averaged across reports nor selected according to outcomes. All extracted measurement cells and raw strings are preserved privately. PREP/CRT demonstrations, planting-date experiments, statewide summaries, and multi-year mean columns are outside the defined ARPT site-year candidate pool.

## Resolved source ambiguities

- The 2019 report PDF page 4 labels its RREC table 2017–2019 in the title but 2016–2018 in the column headings. The entire page is excluded; the intended years are not inferred from matching numerical values.
- The 2017 report PDF page 7 retains a Desha title but its current-year trial is Chicot according to the same report's site list and annual table, corroborated by Research Series 651, printed page 256 / PDF page 258. The 2017 column is assigned to Chicot. Historical 2014–2015 columns remain Desha. Conversely the 2018 Chicot table's 2015 column belongs to the documented earlier Desha trial. The 2016 trial was lost to blackbird damage and no absent 2016 pool is invented.
- The 2021 annual report distinguishes Poinsett POI1 and POI2. Later NERREC historical summaries cannot identify which 2021 site they represent; those later 2021 cells are excluded. Original 2021 annual POI1 and POI2 cells remain separate.
- A 1.2-point font-baseline offset in the 2021 historical tables was resolved before freezing extraction. Five truly empty source cells were visually confirmed and remain missing. Superscript lodging is read using its smaller font, not concatenated into field yield.

## Units and endpoint

Annual 2021–2025 methods state that plot grain weights are adjusted to 12% moisture and milling samples are dried to approximately 12%. The ARPT methods in Research Series 651 (2017), PDF page 258, and Series 667 (2019), PDF page 220, and Series 676 (2020), PDF page 218, independently corroborate the 12% basis and four replicate randomized complete blocks. Research Series 617 (2013), PDF page 77, states three replicates during 2010–2012 and the increase to four in 2013; Series 626 (2014), PDF page 250, corroborates four replicates and 12% moisture. Replicate-level values are unavailable in the annual tables.

Research Series 714 (2024), PDF page 190 / printed page 188, explicitly gives both the ARPT 12% moisture basis and 45 pounds per bushel; Series 705 (2023), PDF page 162 / printed page 160, corroborates them. The conversion is `bu/ac × 45 × 0.45359237 / 0.40468564224 = kg/ha`. The separate observed test-weight column, commonly around 40 lb/bu, measures bulk density and is not used as the yield conversion. General commercial guidance at 13% moisture is not the trial basis. All final selected field-yield values are integer bu/ac; milling recoveries are integer percentage points. The conversion preserves the published precision without inventing additional measurement precision.

The official Rice Production Handbook, chapter 14, PDF page 1 / printed page 169, defines both total milling yield and head rice yield using original rough-rice mass as denominator. Head kernels are at least three quarters of original kernel length. Thus HR and TR are separate percentages, not a head fraction of already-milled rice. The research endpoint is `mean field yield × mean HR / 100`. It is the product of two published cultivar means, not a recovered mean plotwise product or a latent true cultivar effect.

## Prespecified source flags and checks

Primary eligibility excludes six source-documented compromised pools: 2022 CLAY and LAW (weeds), 2022 PTRS (low harvest moisture), 2024 NEREC (bird damage), 2024 ARK (volunteer rice), and 2025 RREC (delayed harvest). All rows remain available with sensitivity flags. Lodging alone does not exclude candidates or pools. These are metadata decisions made without policy-performance comparisons. The entire 2025 NERREC pool is flagged previously seen, regardless of cultivar or measurement availability.

`arkansas_coverage.csv` provides per-pool candidates, observed grain, paired availability, eligible counts, grain classes, damage reasons, previously-seen status, and the n≥10 eligibility flag. `arkansas_year_screen.csv` distinguishes annual table compatibility from later recovered historical labels. `public_features.csv` contains no HR, TR, or product outcome. Those fields are isolated in `private_outcomes.csv` with exactly matching row IDs.

Verification uses a second Poppler layout/token extraction against pdfplumber word geometry for every extracted measurement cell, including repeated cells and missing labels. Five visually verified blank cells receive explicit empty-token positions in the independent parser. Rendered-page checks cover current grain and milling joins, superscripts, the missing cells, year ambiguity, the damage footnotes, and methods/denominators. Numeric visual checks are quarantined under `derived/arkansas/private_visual_checks`. Sixteen focused tests cover identity, missingness, source precedence, site aliases, ambiguous years, units, outcome separation, lodging, and source hashes. Clean repeated preparation must reproduce all deterministic CSV outputs byte for byte; timestamps in the preparation manifest are not compared.

Rebuild locally with `.venv/bin/python src/acquire_arkansas.py --offline`, `.venv/bin/python src/prepare_arkansas.py`, and `.venv/bin/python src/prepare_arkansas.py --verify-only`. Then run `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_arkansas_extraction.py`.
