# Decision-focused prior-art audit

Audit date: 20 November 2026. This is a bounded primary-source review, not a systematic review or proof that no closer work exists. The [closest-work table](prior_art_closest_work.csv) records 11 studies, their decision, information, budget, evaluation, overlap, access limits and local evidence files. DOI identities were checked against Crossref and primary publisher/repository records.

Sequential phenotyping and selection-focused evaluation are established. [Tanaka and Iwata](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00122-017-2988-z) introduced expected-improvement search for the best genotype. [Tsai et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13253-021-00454-2) developed repeated phenotype batches, uncertainty-based acquisition and stopping rules. [Tu and Liao](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13253-024-00632-y), [Chen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00122-024-04766-y) and [Liu and Liao](https://doi.org/10.1093/g3journal/jkag031) evaluate limited training sets using top-genotype or ranking objectives, including rice and multiple environments. These papers rule out novelty claims based only on a phenotyping budget, exploration/exploitation, top-k ranking, or using selection metrics instead of prediction error.

The rice-specific overlap is also substantial. [Scheffel et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/csc2.70211) use historical Uruguay data with availability-aware early selection. Their reference breeding/genetic values are fitted to the whole archive; this is a different evaluation target from measured candidate output. [Monteverde et al.](https://doi.org/10.2135/cropsci2017.09.0564) already analyze multi-environment rice yield and milling traits with mixed/genomic models. [Dhakal et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/tpg2.70068) predict head recovery using markers and secondary quality traits. [Clarke et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs16101815) forecast head-rice yield using climate and remote sensing with year-based validation. Some inputs in those studies would be unavailable before the milling decision here.

The defensible proposed addition is a specific empirical decision study: after field harvest, choose which entry-level milling results to request, then shortlist only measured entries by known field yield times head recovery. Its distinguishing evidence would be a temporal replay with auditable query gates, a matched first-batch update-withheld comparison, and transfer between independently acquired programs when actually executable. This is a proposed empirical addition, not a new general acquisition principle or demonstrated laboratory improvement. A gain over yield-first alone is insufficient: the adaptive policy must be compared with the strongest development-selected static model and its identical-first-batch no-update counterpart. A null or adverse feedback effect should remain a main finding.

The search log records 30 queries and 130 returned occurrences, including duplicate routes. Of these, 112 occurrences covering 103 distinct URLs were serialized and screened. The initial four queries returned 18 occurrences recorded in the conversation but were not serialized; the three seed papers were subsequently independently verified. This limitation is explicit in [the log](prior_art_search_log.json). Twenty-one archived occurrences remain adjacent methods/measurement leads without full-text review; they are not evidence of absence. The audit stopped after this bounded search and source verification. It did not claim global saturation.

Full public text was saved for the central Tsai, Tu, Chen, Liu, Monteverde, Dhakal, Clarke and image-phenotyping papers. Tanaka remains a subscription-preview limitation. Wiley pages for the two Uruguay seed papers were verifiable through indexed primary methods, while direct byte downloads returned403. HTTP200 was not treated as proof of full text: challenge pages are identified in the table. Search snippets inadvertently exposed Arkansas report material; no candidate outcomes or policy comparisons were passed to the modeling agent. Raw search files should remain outside the model-development reading path until the outcome freeze is released.

The [source manifest](prior_art_file_manifest.json) hashes downloaded evidence and audit scripts. The original acquisition manifests retain attempted public routes, HTTP failures and timeouts. No authors were contacted, and no paid or restricted access was bypassed.
