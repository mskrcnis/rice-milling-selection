# Source-specific unit and denominator audit

As of 20 November 2026, Uruguay's recovered Dryad README describes GY in kg/ha and PHR as a percentage of milled whole kernels. It does not explicitly state the denominator or a common grain-yield moisture standard. The data paper, [Rebollo et al.2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/csc2.20955), reports trait consolidation but does not establish that missing detail in the accessible methods/Table1.

[Monteverde et al.2018](https://doi.org/10.2135/cropsci2017.09.0564), from the same INIA program and its2010–12 UEPL trials, explicitly defines PHR as whole milled kernel mass divided by rough-rice mass using a100g sample (methods p1521; local `raw/literature/monteverde2018_publicpdf.txt`, lines146–153). This supports the rough-rice interpretation for the archive. It is an inference across program records, not direct verification of every source year, laboratory or conversion. The recovered README should be retained beside this source evidence, and the assumption stated in the report. Archivewide moisture remains unresolved.

The Arkansas source auditor verified that the annual performance-trial methods report grain yield in bushels/acre adjusted to12% moisture. The2024 B.R.Wells Research Series714 methods additionally define a bushel as45lb. The general Extension [FSA-1074 drying guide](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/publications/PDF/FSA-1074.pdf), which also uses45lb/bu, gives13% as a drying standard; it must not override the trial-specific12% convention. The annual methods and milling-definition evidence are maintained by the Arkansas source auditor in `raw/arkansas` and the Arkansas audit artifacts.

At45lb/bu, the exact area/mass conversion is `45*0.45359237/0.40468564224` kg/ha per bu/ac. This conversion changes units, not moisture basis. For otherwise compatible material, changing grain mass from moisture m1 to m2 would multiply it by `(100-m1)/(100-m2)`; do not perform that correction when the source basis is unknown. The Colombian optimal-harvest sample's13% preparation in [Dhakal et al.2025](https://doi.org/10.1002/tpg2.70068) is not evidence of the Uruguay or Arkansas convention.

When HR is a rough-rice percentage, field-yield mass times HR/100 is the defined whole-kernel-output proxy. Do not multiply total milling recovery again. If another source defines HR relative to total milled rice, total recovery would also be required. Product of candidate-level means differs from mean of paired plotwise products; preserve the declared definition and a matched-plot sensitivity when support permits. The unit conversion does not turn a retrospective proxy into a directly observed hectare-scale milled output.

Cross-program absolute outputs should retain their source moisture/laboratory basis, with no claim of harmonized physical measurements. Relative regret and within-pool rankings are unchanged by a common positive mass-conversion factor, but this does not remove laboratory, cultivar, missingness or moisture-related changes in recovery itself. Program-specific measurement differences still matter for transfer of a recovery predictor.
